---------------------------
	show a Size column in the Backup page download tables (deferred from the
		backup_page_redesign feature, docs/plans/backup_page_redesign_*)
	what is knowable ahead of time:
		SQLite index: exact (filesize of the served file; can go stale)
		image archive: payload sum from ImageStore inventory, "about X"
