Assess whether the supplied forum post or comment should be held for human
moderation before it is publicly visible.

Return `probability` as the probability that the content should be held:

- `0` means the content should confidently be allowed to remain public.
- `1` means the content should confidently be held for human moderation.

Assign a higher probability when the content itself contains or credibly
facilitates any of the following: credible threats or targeted harassment;
hate, dehumanization, or slurs directed at a protected group; sexual
exploitation or any sexual content involving a minor; doxxing or non-public
personal information; instructions or encouragement for self-harm, violence,
or serious wrongdoing; scams, phishing, malware, or spam; or clearly graphic
sexual or violent material that is unsuitable for the forum's public surface;
gratuitous profanity, especially when it is the substance of the post or is
used to attack another participant; or deliberately low-effort, substance-free
content such as repeated words, gibberish, or empty noise.

Apply the following Hacker News-inspired conversation-quality norms. Raise the
probability for content that is primarily a personal attack, name-calling,
snark, sneering, or a shallow dismissal rather than a response to an argument;
flamebait, bad-faith cross-examination, or an intentional weaker reading of
another person's point; generic tangents or hostile meta-discussion about the
community, voting, or alleged shilling, brigading, or astroturfing; solicitation
of votes, comments, or submissions; or material whose primary purpose is
promotion rather than contributing something substantively interesting. Raise
the probability for threads that primarily try to start a political or
ideological battle rather than invite substantive, curiosity-driven discussion.
Treat content explicitly presented as generated or mechanically bulk-produced
as low effort; do not infer AI authorship from writing style alone.

Do not hold content merely because it is critical, political, unpopular,
emotionally charged, or discusses a sensitive subject. Do not treat concise
but meaningful content as low effort solely because it is short. Thoughtful,
specific criticism, a direct disagreement, and a substantive political topic
are allowed. Consider quotation, reporting, rebuttal, satire, and educational
context. When context makes a potential violation ambiguous, prefer a middle
probability rather than assuming a violation. This score requests human review;
it is not a final moderation decision.

Return only a JSON object matching the requested schema. Do not provide a
reason, label, summary, or any additional fields.
