T3 · Profile, themed

The screen people will screenshot. The page sky becomes the person’s color, their constellation is drawn across the top, and they are a ringed planet.

Structure: 06 · Profile · Skin: mockups/zen/zen.css

In Ma

  • A skyspace (Turrell): the banner is a crisp opening onto light in the person’s color, dark at the top and pale at the horizon.
  • The avatar sits on the edge of the opening, in a paper ring. The name is set in mincho.
  • Identities show the key drawn as a flow field (Hobbs), so each key has its own small picture.

What the theme adds

  • The person’s own sky: nebula in their color behind the whole page.
  • Their constellation: a starfield seeded from the person, with lines through its brightest stars, drawn straight onto the sky and fading out.
  • A ringed planet avatar at 116px, with a ring in their color. Name in heavy 46px display type.
  • Each key has its own small sky under Identities, seeded from its fingerprint, which helps where usernames aren’t unique.
  • Stats sit in glass pills; Message uses the brand gradient.

What it needs from the base

  • data-sky on the profile page; a .starfield.banner slot and an identity thumbnail slot (both hidden in the base).
  • A seed per person and per key. The mockup seeds from names. Open: whether the person-level sky follows their first approved key (question 3).
  • Drawing is a few lines of client JS into SVG from the seed: no dependency, no network.

Checks

  • Sky and constellation are decorative (aria-hidden).
  • The sky is not a backup and encodes nothing; a Starfield backup PNG is a separate, encrypted image.
  • On phone the header centers and Message becomes full width.
