T6 · Conversation, themed

A conversation happens under the other person’s sky. Their messages carry their color; yours carry the brand gradient.

Structure: 10 · Conversation · Skin: mockups/zen/zen.css

In Ma

  • At night, the newest incoming message visibly decrypts: it scrambles through hex and resolves once when it scrolls into view. Short failure labels glitch; the encryption line is set in a data typeface.
  • Their bubbles are tinted paper in their color; yours are ink on paper (paper on ink in dark mode). Vermilion is kept for seals and actions.
  • First message: the other person’s key, drawn as a flow field, in a paper circle.
  • The page is lit from above in the other person’s color (Turrell).

What the theme adds

  • Their sky: the nebula behind the conversation is their color.
  • Their bubbles are glass tinted with their color and a soft halo. Yours use the gradient.
  • A first message opens on their world: their planet inside a glowing orb of their stars.
  • Failure states use plain glass and danger text, never a person’s color.

What it needs from the base

  • data-sky and --person on the conversation, set from the other participant.
  • A starfield slot behind the avatar in the empty state.

Checks

  • Their bubbles keep ink text at 12:1 or better for every avatar color.
  • White on the gradient bubble is 4.6:1 or better at every stop.
