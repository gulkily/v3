T1 · Board, themed

The Board under a live sky. Each thread is lit by its author’s color, so the list reads as a field of people, not rows.

Structure: 02 · Board · Skin: mockups/zen/zen.css

In Ma

  • At night (dark mode), the logo is a neon sign that flickers once, rain replaces petals, and times and counts use a data typeface.
  • Paper and space (Hara): one sheet of paper for the list, hairlines, no glass and no blur. Titles in a quiet mincho serif.
  • One shaft of light (Ando) crosses the page and moves as slowly as the sun. The page is lit from above in a soft field of color (Turrell).
  • Petals for the pulse (teamLab): on a busy day seven petals fall; on an ordinary day three; on a quiet day none, and the light holds still.
  • People are flat shapes (Tanaka): each avatar is a circle with its own cut (half, quarter or a small moon), fixed per name.
  • The logo is an ensō that is also a calm face (Fukuda): a zen circle and a meme in one mark, next to a vermilion seal. Liked-empty: the face looks sideways at the star that isn’t there yet.

What the theme adds

  • The screen is a sky. Dawn gradient in light mode; deep space with drifting nebulae, twinkling stars and an occasional shooting star in dark. Motion is slow (26–39 s cycles) and stops under reduced motion.
  • Frosted glass for every surface, so color shows through around content but never behind text at full strength.
  • Author glow: each row has a soft halo in its author’s color behind the avatar. Avatars are small glowing planets.
  • Gradient as the brand: wordmark, New post, unread dots, badges and hot reply counts share one ember → magenta → violet gradient.
  • Colored dots on the navigation (Board blue, Users green, Messages pink). Pinned threads get a gold rim.
  • Ensō logo: a one-stroke zen circle in the brand gradient replaces the star mark (desktop top bar).
  • Community pulse: the sky reflects the last 24 hours. On a busy day nebulae are brighter and shooting stars come more often; on a quiet day the sky dims and holds still. Compare the first and second screens in dark mode.

What it needs from the base

  • A sky layer as the first child of the app shell (decorative, aria-hidden).
  • --person on each thread row; data-tab on navigation items; an empty .mark slot in the wordmark.
  • Heart → star icon swap for Like.
  • data-pulse (quiet, steady or busy) on the app shell, from one cached count of posts in the last 24 hours.

Checks

  • Text never sits directly on the moving sky except titles and section labels, which use ink at 6:1 or better on the lightest sky color.
  • White on every gradient stop is 4.6:1 or better.
  • Glass is 62% opaque, so the sky can’t lower body-text contrast below AA.
