T4 · Account, themed

Getting an identity should feel like an event. The account card is a night sky in both modes, lit by your color once you have one.

Structure: 08 · Account · Skin: mockups/zen/zen.css

In Ma

  • At night, the key art glows like neon tubing, and the card reads like an ID chip: corner brackets and a caption of real key data.
  • Your key, drawn (Hobbs): the account card opens with a flow field generated from your key, mostly in your color with some ink and, rarely, one vermilion stroke.
  • Creating the account: the ensō face closes its eyes while a brush goes round.
  • The backup sheet’s Starfield thumbnail keeps its stars, because that is what the file looks like.

What the theme adds

  • A night card on any sky: deep space with your seeded stars and constellation, glowing in your color. In light mode it sits on the dawn sky like a window.
  • Colored glyphs on rows: Back up (blue), Restore (teal), Advanced (slate), Remove (red). No colored tiles; the icon carries the color.
  • The backup format choice is visual: “Starfield image” shows a small starfield.
  • Orbit loader: while the key is generated, your planet appears in the card with a moon circling it (second screen). The same loader fits restoring a backup and decrypting messages.
  • Warnings keep plain danger styling; the theme never decorates them.

What it needs from the base

  • A night class and starfield slot on the account card; data-sky on the page; data-tone on rows.

Checks

  • Text on the night card is white or 80% white on near-black: well above AA.
  • Row glyphs are 4.1:1 or better on glass in both modes (icon minimum 3:1).
  • The orbit is decorative; the heading is a status message and the card is marked busy. Under reduced motion the moon holds still.
