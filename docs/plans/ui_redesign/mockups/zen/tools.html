T5 · Tools, themed

Colored icons, as asked: the glyph itself carries the color, the way avatars do. Each tool’s color is fixed, and groups share related shades.

Structure: 11 · Tools · Skin: mockups/zen/zen.css

In Ma

  • Flat colored glyphs: each icon is drawn in its tool’s color, with closed shapes filled flat. No glow.
  • Each group has a shape (Tanaka): Your device a circle, Reading a square, Community a triangle, Operator a half-circle. Shape and color both mark the group.
  • Tool names in mincho; paper tiles with hairlines.

What the theme adds

  • Colored glyphs, not colored tiles. Each icon is drawn in its tool’s color, with closed shapes lightly filled for a duotone look and a soft glow in the same color.
  • Group shades: Your device in blues, Reading in pinks and violets, Community in greens, Operator in ambers and stones. Each group heading has a glowing dot.
  • Glass tiles carry a faint wash of the tool’s color in one corner and light up in that color on hover.
  • The same glyph colors appear in the account menu, Account rows and the phone tab bar.

What it needs from the base

  • A stable data-tone key per tool, set in ToolsPageSupport, and on the group heading.
  • Colors live in the theme. Icon themes (Word97, Chicago, Midnight) keep their own icons in theme-nav-icons.css.

Checks

  • Every tool keeps a distinct glyph and a label; color is a second signal.
  • Glyph contrast is 3.6:1 or better on light glass and 4.5:1 or better on dark (dark mode lightens each tone 35%).
  • The Operator tones are the least distinct, on purpose: few people see that group.
