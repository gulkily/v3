The home page and the most visited screen. Its job is to show what is new and make posting one tap away.

Wording

  • Tags · All · Liked · Newest · Oldest · Top · New PostAll | Liked segmented control, a “Newest ▾” sort menu and a Tags link. The New Post button moves to the app bar so it isn’t shown twice.
  • by ilyag (unapproved) on Oct 6, 2026 at 12:37 UTCilyag · Oct 6 · a “Pending” chip. The full UTC timestamp moves to a tooltip and long-press.
  • 6 repliesA reply count in its own column, so you can scan the right edge for busy threads.
  • Start a thread…What’s on your mind?
  • PINNEDA “Pinned” section header with a pin icon, instead of a badge on each pinned row.

Arrangement

  • Threads sit in one list separated by hairlines, not a stack of boxed cards. About three times as many threads fit above the fold: 9 rows on desktop instead of 3.
  • Rows show an avatar, the title, a one-line excerpt (only when the body adds something beyond the title), metadata and the count.
  • Today both Liked and Newest render as “pressed” buttons. Filter and sort become two separate controls, so the current view is unambiguous.
  • On phone, the filter row stays on one line and the tab bar replaces the five wrapping nav buttons.

Flow

  • The quick-compose row opens the full composer (mockup 4) rather than expanding a second, inline copy of the form inside the list.
  • Pagination becomes “Show older threads”, which loads more into the same list. This follows the pagination contract in docs/reference/pagination.md, so the position stays in the URL.
  • An empty Liked view explains itself and offers a way back to All.
  • Proposal: show the Pending chip only to the author and to approvers. Today everyone sees “(unapproved)”. This changes access behavior, so it needs a product decision.
