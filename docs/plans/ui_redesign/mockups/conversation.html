10 · Conversation

One private conversation. Every message is encrypted, signed and verified in the browser, and the interface should only bring that up when something goes wrong.

templates/pages/private_message_conversation.php · partials/private_message_composer.php · partials/private_message_item.php

Wording

  • Conversation with onthebus · Back to Messagesonthebus, as the title, with a back chevron.
  • Encrypted to all approved keys. / Encrypted to every approved key associated with this username.“Only you and onthebus can read these, on your approved devices.” It says who can read the messages, in plain terms, and stays visible because it is the privacy promise.
  • Send private messageA send arrow button with the accessible name “Send”.
  • Check previous send“Not confirmed · Check” under the bubble it applies to.
  • Message unavailable · Details / Retry reading message“Couldn’t verify this message” bubble, with Details and Retry.
  • Load older · Restart history · Reopen conversationOlder messages load as you scroll up. Restart history is under ··· because it is a recovery tool.

Arrangement

  • Bubbles: yours on the right in the accent color, theirs on the left. Day separators replace the per-message “Sent: 2026-10-23T19:30:10Z”.
  • “Seen” appears under your last message only.
  • The signature check mark (✓ “Signature verified”) disappears from every message. Verification is the default, so only failures are shown.
  • The composer is pinned above the keyboard, as on the thread page.

Flow

  • Sending shows the bubble immediately at reduced opacity, then full once delivered. A failure shows “Not sent · Retry” on that bubble and keeps the text.
  • An uncertain send (network dropped after submit) shows “Not confirmed · Check”. Today this is a detached button.
  • Offline: the composer says “You’re offline. Messages send when you reconnect.” Proposal: queue private messages to the Outbox like posts. Check that the offline queue supports encrypted messages before promising this.
