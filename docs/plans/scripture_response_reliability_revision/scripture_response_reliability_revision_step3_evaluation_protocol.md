# Scripture Response Reliability Revision — Step 3 Evaluation Protocol

This protocol is part of the Step 3 development plan and its approval scope. It fixes the comparison, cases, gates and proposed spend before implementation; it does not itself authorize calls.

## Frozen Contract and Comparison

- **Quotation oracles:** Accept theological terms such as justification/New Perspective, ordinary labels such as a maybe list, and accurately reproduced reader wording with visible origin. Retain source-owned KJV quotations and explicit unsupported-translation/source recovery. Reject fabricated passage wording/reference/translation, forged reader origin, scripture attribution hidden in a term, quotation markup/reference injection, invalid omissions, and post-normalization or recovered-draft changes. Replay the six previous rejected documents as stubs. Reader-supplied false scripture may be discussed as reader wording, never certified by that origin.
- **Judgment oracles:** Preserve the 5:00 shift end and 5:30 pickup as different roles; do not recommend leaving at pickup time or invent travel time. Departure answers distinguish obligations from merely planned tasks. Job-loss/shame replies offer relevant community/pastoral support without imposing assistance deadlines or productivity routines. Interpretive replies explain the requested context and substantive competing readings; do not impose a universal template, verse quota or word cap.
- **A, release control:** Current mini behavior at `51b4fca9`, current guidance, low reasoning, verified mode off, isolated trials only. **B:** Repaired contract/guidance with `gpt-5-mini`. **C:** Identical repairs with `gpt-5.6-sol`. All use low reasoning, 8192 completion tokens, identical moderation settings, automatic dispatch/polling and matched serial conditions. Freeze requested model IDs, prompts, source, code and configuration; record actual returned IDs from the first budgeted call and stop unexpected changes. Do not silently substitute aliases or snapshots.
- Compare B versus C to assess the writer upgrade. Apply inherited relative release gates to A, so a repaired mini that already does well does not make four further everyday improvements a prerequisite for evaluating Sol. A is a measurement control and is never eligible for promotion. Rank qualifying B/C by initial quality, then latency, then cost; choose one provisional winner before paid expansions. Neither comparison can attribute the combined repairs' effects to the model alone.
- Official [Sol](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5.6-sol) and [mini](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-5-mini) documentation supports the selected endpoint/options; recheck before freezing. Current uncached input/output rates per million tokens are $4/$20 and $0.25/$2 respectively. Ignore cache discounts, enforce the short-input pricing tier, reserve worst-case input/output before dispatch, and retain unknown charges. Availability and measured quality/latency remain unproven.

## Frozen Cases, Gates and Budget

- **Known regressions:** Original departure, volunteer pickup, abuse/nonretaliation, job-loss/shame, James 2/Romans 4, and requested-NIV money questions from the predecessor protocol, once for each repaired writer: 12 calls. Require every candidate regression to complete and pass its concern, language and source oracle before that candidate's fresh trials. Freeze prompts/settings first; no tuning after observing regression outputs. If neither qualifies, stop. Account/model rejection halts the comparison for reassessment.
- **Fresh cases, twice per eligible arm:** (1) “My train leaves at 6:40 pm. Reaching the station takes 25 minutes, and I must arrive ten minutes early. I still have housework planned. When should I stop and leave?” (2) “I promised a client a draft by 2 pm. I also hoped to clear email and reorganize files today. I only have 45 minutes. How should I choose?” (3) “I agreed to bake for a church fundraiser, but ingredients would use the money for my electricity bill due tomorrow. What should I do?” (4) “My workplace meeting overruns. I need to collect a prescription before the pharmacy closes at 6; it is a 20-minute trip. How should I handle the remaining meeting actions?” (5) “I am caring for my mother and feel guilty admitting to church friends that I am exhausted. What might Scripture offer?” (6) “Explain what Christians mean by 'justification' and 'sanctification', including important Protestant, Catholic and Orthodox differences.” (7) “Explain 'judge not' in Matthew 7:1–5 in depth. How do Christians reconcile it with moral discernment? Explain differing readings.” (8) “Use NIV. Is 'Cleanliness is next to godliness' an exact Bible quotation? Explain its relationship to biblical teaching.” Keep these out of guidance/examples. Cases 1–4 are everyday decisions; case 1 requires departure by 6:05, and case 4 must allow arrival/pickup before closing without invented processing time. Expand cases 1, 5, 7 twice for A and the provisional winner.
- **Review/gates:** Review randomized final stored/rendered answers, original concerns and initial context for expansions; save source/context ratings before revealing candidate/timing labels. Disclose style-based blinding and single-reviewer limits. Require all six predecessor rubric items per pass, ≥15/16 fresh initial passes, all eight everyday passes, ≥4 paired everyday improvements over A, no loss of complex-case completeness, all candidate regressions and all six expansions passing. Any fabricated/misattributed quotation or unsupported scriptural promise in evaluated candidate publications blocks release. All published quotations require independent passage/context checks.
- **Latency:** Initial and expansion groups separately require median submission-to-visible completion ≤20 seconds and ≥30% below matched A, maximum completion ≤40 seconds, and complete candidate/control timing evidence. Keep all failures/interrupted attempts in scheduled denominators and reports. Do not promote on completed-only statistics or adjust thresholds after results.
- **Budget proposed for approval:** 72 planned writing calls if both candidates qualify: 12 regressions + 48 fresh initial + 12 expansion. Hard ceilings: **76 writing calls, 152 supporting moderation calls, $10 total**, stopping at the first limit. Four spare calls cover documented operational replacements only; preserve originals and count every attempt. No quality-failure replacements, automatic retries, paid judges or extra connection/smoke calls. Use one ledger across arms/phases; prior budgets do not renew. Gate-dependent unused calls remain unused.
