# Pledge campaign

Purpose: Let a thread ask for an amount of something in a unit its author picks, and let replies promise part of it, with no money moving through the site.
Scenario: With `FORUM_PLEDGES_ENABLED` on, Ilya posts a thread whose opening post has the line `goal: 1000 coffees`. The thread page shows "0 of 1000 coffees pledged" and a bar under the opening post. Mara enters 300 in the Pledge field above the reply box and posts; her reply's first line is `pledge: 300`, the reply shows "300 coffees", and the block shows "300 of 1000 coffees pledged · 1 pledge". An agent replies with the line `pledge: 12.50` and the sum becomes 312.5. Once the sum passes the goal the bar is full and a mark shows where the goal sits. Rin replies `pledge: a few`; the reply stays as written and shows "Not counted: “a few” is not a number". Retracting a pledge takes it out of the sum. With the flag off the lines are plain text.
Sources: [goal line](../../src/ForumRewrite/Support/GoalDirective.php), [pledge line](../../src/ForumRewrite/Support/PledgeDirective.php), [amount rules](../../src/ForumRewrite/Support/PledgeAmount.php), [totals reader](../../src/ForumRewrite/View/PledgeCampaign.php), [totals block](../../templates/partials/pledge_totals.php), [amount on a reply](../../templates/partials/pledge_amount.php), [amount field](../../templates/partials/reply_form.php) and [its script](../../public/assets/pledge_field.js), [tests](../../tests/PledgeCampaignTest.php), [browser check](../../tests/browser/pledge_campaign.mjs).

Derived, not stored: `PledgeCampaign::fromPosts()` reads the lines from the rows the thread page already loads, so a rebuild cannot disagree with the posts and a new reply's baked page carries the new sum.
Numbers: digits with an optional decimal point, at most two decimal places, above zero; commas only as thousands grouping (`1,000`). The goal line is a number, then the unit as free text. A pledge line may repeat the unit; other trailing text leaves it uncounted.
Counting: a pledge counts when its post is unretracted, names its author and has counted attribution. A post with no name (which includes every post made without JavaScript) and, while Verified attribution is on, an unverified post show their amount struck through with the reason, and stay out of the sum. Approval is not required. Every reader sees the same reason. With `FORUM_PLEDGES_COUNT_UNNAMED_ENABLED` ("Count pledges with no name", off by default, needs Pledges on) a post with no name counts like any other; unverified posts still do not.
Amount field: only on the campaign's thread page. The script keeps the first line `pledge: N` in the body in step with the field, so signed, unsigned and Outbox sending carry it unchanged; without JavaScript the server writes the line. The separate Reply page and the paned layouts show the lines as text and have no field.
