# Post presentation and visibility

Purpose: Present public contributions with existing author attribution, readable content and links to their source conversation.
Scenario: A Recent preview reuses shared author/time metadata and plain-text title/excerpt handling, then links to the thread, anchored to the reply; a post whose thread is unavailable links to its own page. Visible replies can remain readable after their thread root becomes unavailable; the preview does not expose excluded root text.
Signals: Every thread root and reply card carries one server-rendered line, `Liked by a, b, c +N · Flagged by d · Title set by e`, naming the reactions the score counts and the identity behind the current title; it is hidden when empty and identical for every viewer, and the viewer's own saved, counted reaction appends their name in place.
Sources: [signals partial](../../templates/partials/post_signals.php), [signal actors](../../src/ForumRewrite/ReadModel/SignalActors.php), [Recent entry](../../templates/partials/recent_entry.php), [renderer metadata helpers](../../src/ForumRewrite/View/TemplateRenderer.php), [title handling](../../src/ForumRewrite/Support/ThreadTitle.php), [thread reader](../../src/ForumRewrite/ReadModel/ThreadRepository.php), [Recent eligibility](../../src/ForumRewrite/ReadModel/RecentPostRepository.php).
Boundary: Recent does not change attribution, approval, post deletion or retraction policy. Profile-history counting rules are not a public-contribution membership filter.
Thumbnails: on the board's list rows and Recent rows, a post with a stored attached image shows it as a small thumbnail as tall as the row, linking where the title does. The page's image references come from one lookup by post ID; a retracted post, a missing file, images turned off (`FORUM_IMAGE_CONTENT_ENABLED`), or list thumbnails turned off (`FORUM_LIST_THUMBNAILS_ENABLED`) show none. The whole image is shown, letterboxed rather than cropped. Rows link small cached copies (fitting 64 x 128 px, plus a 2x copy) at `/images/thumbs/<sha256>-<size>.<ext>` (`1x`, `2x`), generated with GD on first request into `<image root>-thumbs` and served only while the original is present; without GD, or when the original is already that small, the original is used. Sources: [list thumbnails](../../src/ForumRewrite/Images/ListThumbnails.php), [row thumbnail](../../templates/partials/row_thumbnail.php), [thumbnail store](../../src/ForumRewrite/Images/ThumbnailStore.php).

Gallery: `/gallery/` lists the same posts as Recent that carry a stored image, newest first, as tiles in justified rows that keep each image's proportions (clamped between 0.6 and 2.2). A tile shows a preview, the thread title and the thread's reply count, and opens the post in its thread. Previews are the thumbnail store's tile copies (fitting 480 x 480 px) at `/images/thumbs/<sha256>-tile.<ext>`, generated and served like row thumbnails. The page and its nav item follow `FORUM_IMAGE_GALLERY_ENABLED`, which requires image attachments. Sources: [gallery](../../templates/pages/gallery.php), [thumbnail store](../../src/ForumRewrite/Images/ThumbnailStore.php).
Formatting: full-post views render a post's body according to its recorded format ([post body formatting](post_body_formatting.md)); previews, quotes and titles always use plain words.
Resolution: on a reply in a thread whose tag unlocks a state ([tagset](tagset.md)), the card wraps its content in `<details>`; resolved and closed replies fold to one line (state, author, date, first words) that expands on tap, also without JavaScript.
