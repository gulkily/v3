# Guestbook

Purpose: Show one thread as a paper book that visitors sign, while the thread stays an ordinary thread for anyone who prefers it.
Scenario: The `zenmemes` profile names its guestbook thread in `guestbookThread`. A visitor opens `/guestbook/` and sees the newest two facing pages; earlier pages sit at `/guestbook/?page=N`. They write on the last page and press Sign the book; the note becomes a top-level reply and they land back on the book. On the thread, Open as a book leads back; a reply to an entry stays in the thread, and the entry shows "1 reply".
Sources: [controller](../../src/ForumRewrite/Http/GuestbookPageController.php), [template](../../templates/pages/guestbook.php), [stylesheet](../../public/assets/guestbook.css), [site profiles](../../src/ForumRewrite/SiteProfileRegistry.php) (`guestbookThread`), [reply return](../../src/ForumRewrite/Http/ComposeAndAccountKeyController.php) (`resolveComposeReplyReturnTo`), [tests](../../tests/GuestbookPageTest.php), [browser check](../../tests/browser/guestbook.mjs), [plans](../plans/guestbook/guestbook_step3_development_plan.md).

Entries are the thread's unretracted top-level replies, three per page, counted from the oldest, so a page's address keeps its entries. A spread is addressed by its left page; no page means the newest spread, which carries the shared reply form. The page exists while `FORUM_GUESTBOOK_ENABLED` is on and the profile names a thread; a named thread that does not exist yet shows an empty book. The book keeps its paper colors and fonts in every theme. The thread page is baked into static files, so its Open as a book link appears or goes after a rebuild.
