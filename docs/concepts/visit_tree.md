# Visit tree

Purpose: Let a reader see which page led to which, and get back to any earlier branch of their reading, without the site learning anything about them.
Scenario: With `FORUM_VISIT_HISTORY_ENABLED` on (off by default), a reader opens the Board, a thread, goes back, then opens a second thread. Tools → History shows the Board with two branches under it, newest first, each visit with its title and time; a click opens that page. With Beside on, a thread opened in the pane sits under the page it was opened from, marked "beside", and links followed in the pane continue that branch. A second tab started from a link hangs under the page it came from; a tab typed into starts its own branch. Clear empties the tree and the next page starts a new one.
Sources: [recorder](../../public/assets/visit_history.js), [History page and its before-paint tree](../../templates/pages/history.php), [styles](../../public/assets/tools.css) (`.visit-tree`), [route](../../src/ForumRewrite/Application.php) and [controller](../../src/ForumRewrite/Http/ToolsPageController.php) (`history()`), [script wiring](../../src/ForumRewrite/View/TemplateRenderer.php) (`visitHistoryScriptPath`, `renderStandalonePage()`), [flag](../../src/ForumRewrite/Support/FeatureFlags/FeatureFlagRegistry.php), [page test](../../tests/VisitHistoryPageTest.php), [browser test](../../tests/browser/visit_history.mjs), [offline test](../../tests/browser/visit_history_offline.mjs), [plans](../plans/nav_tree/nav_tree_step4_implementation_summary.md).

A visit is a page change: a page load, or a `pushState` to another address (path and query; a `#` spot is the same page, and the one-time hints `__v` and `created_post_id` are dropped). Back and forward, page-cache restores, reloads and loads at the current visit's address move the tab's cursor and add nothing; `replaceState` moves the current visit to the new address. The parent is the tab's current visit, kept in `sessionStorage`; a tab with none uses the latest visit at the referrer. The Beside pane keeps its own cursor in the same tab: [open beside](open_beside.md) tells the recorder whether a pane load is a reader's click or the pane restored on the next main page, and Open alone moves the tab to the pane's visit. Frames other than the pane, such as the [tour](tour_snapshot.md), record nothing. The History page records nothing about itself. Offline, the offline reader's pages are recorded like any other.

Storage is one local-storage key, `forum_visit_history`, holding up to 2,000 visits oldest first (internal browser state as JSON, like [navigation slots](navigation_slots.md)). Visits are only added, each with an ID, so syncing a username's history across devices stays possible later. A visit whose parent was dropped heads its own branch. Full, missing or unreadable storage leaves pages working and History empty; a damaged key is replaced on the next visit. History never leaves the browser and is separate from the opt-in [usage reports](usage_reports.md) log, which strips IDs. Changing or signing out of a key keeps history.

Not covered yet: drawers and in-page tabs as visits, richer tree views and bookmarks (`tree_history`), the filmstrip (`filmstrip`) and syncing; see the [browsing tree checklist](../plans/browsing_tree_checklist.md).
