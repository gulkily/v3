# Date phrase

Purpose: Let a poster say when an event happens in their own words ("next Friday at 7", "the weekend after Thanksgiving"), fill the event's dates and time from them, and keep the words beside the dates.
Scenario: With Event phrases on, a member types `next Thursday at 7` in the composer's When box and presses Fill. The built-in reader reads it with the member's own date: Date becomes next week's Thursday and Time 19:00, in place. The member checks the pickers and posts; the root record carries `Event-When: next Thursday at 7` after the other event headers, and the event block shows `📅 2026-11-19 at 19:00 · “next Thursday at 7”`. A phrase the built-in reader cannot read goes to the configured LLM when the member is approved; an unclear answer, a provider error or no provider changes no field and says so.
Sources: [built-in reader](../../src/ForumRewrite/Calendar/EventPhraseReader.php) (its rules for loose English are at the top), [LLM fallback](../../src/ForumRewrite/Calendar/EventPhraseLlmReader.php), [filling the fields](../../src/ForumRewrite/Calendar/EventPhraseFill.php), [who gets the fallback](../../src/ForumRewrite/Http/EventPhraseFillFactory.php), [read endpoint](../../src/ForumRewrite/Http/EventPhraseApiController.php), [in-place Fill](../../public/assets/event_when.js), [record header](../specs/canonical_post_record_v1.md#event-headers), [phrase test set](../../tests/EventPhraseReaderTest.php), [browser test](../../tests/browser/event_when.mjs).
Boundary: Behind `FORUM_EVENT_WHEN_ENABLED`, which needs event support; off, the box, the header and the shown phrase are gone and a stored phrase stays in its record. The dates remain the authority: the calendar reads them, never the phrase, and a phrase needs a date to be posted. Fill never posts and never changes a fixed Date. Without JavaScript Fill reads with the UTC date and says so. Fill calls are not recorded in the LLM exchange store. English only; agents send dates, and may add the phrase, but the server does not read a phrase sent alone.
