# Media embed

Purpose: Turn a recognized YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, Bandcamp or TikTok link in a post into one calm, playable object, without loading the provider until someone asks to play.
Scenario: A post holds a YouTube link. With `FORUM_MEDIA_EMBEDS_ENABLED` and `FORUM_MEDIA_EMBEDS_INLINE_PLAYER_ENABLED` on, it renders as one bar: a round play control with "Play", and the provider link ("YouTube ↗", labelled "Open on YouTube") at the bar's end. One tap opens the player under the bar and asks it to start (YouTube and TikTok `autoplay=1`, SoundCloud `auto_play=true`; Spotify and Bandcamp have no such setting). The bar then says Close. Opening a player by tap closes any other on the page, and closing a player clears its frame so it stops. With `FORUM_MEDIA_EMBEDS_POSTERS_ENABLED` also on, a closed YouTube player (not a Short) is its poster at 16:9 with the play control over it. The poster is copied once from YouTube's image host by a hidden background request on the first view, then served from this site, so page views never contact YouTube. On `/music/` a closed player is a small tile beside the track's title ([music section](music_section.md)).
Sources: [renderer](../../src/ForumRewrite/View/MediaEmbedRenderer.php), [player script](../../public/assets/media_embed_inline_player.js), [poster store](../../src/ForumRewrite/View/MediaEmbedPosterStore.php), [poster request and route](../../src/ForumRewrite/Http/MediaEmbedPreviewController.php), [styles](../../public/assets/site.css) (`.media-embed-player`, `.music-tracks`), [flags](../../src/ForumRewrite/Support/FeatureFlags/FeatureFlagRegistry.php), [tests](../../tests/MediaEmbedRendererTest.php), [poster tests](../../tests/MediaEmbedPosterStoreTest.php), [browser check](../../tests/browser/media_embed_browser.mjs), [plans](../plans/embed_presentation/embed_presentation_step3_development_plan.md), [earlier embed work](../plans/media_embed_support_checklist.md).

Boundary: no provider frame loads before a tap, and a page never fetches a poster while it renders. Posters live in a `posters` folder beside the preview cache database, not in the image store: they are a disposable cache, deleting them is safe, and a failed fetch waits an hour before the next try. A poster is answered from `/internal/media-embeds/posters/<video id>.jpg` with the one-year header attached images use. Without JavaScript the bar still opens the player, without autostart or closing others. Instagram keeps its preview card. Offline mode does not render embeds.
