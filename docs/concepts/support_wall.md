# Support wall

Purpose: Show one pledge campaign as a wall of squares, one per unit, so a visitor sees how much is covered and by whom, and can cover several squares in one pledge.
Scenario: With `FORUM_PLEDGES_ENABLED` on, the `zenmemes` profile names its support thread in `supportThread`, a thread whose opening post ends with `goal: 50 squares`. A visitor opens `/support/` and sees "Support ZenMemes", the opening post as the pitch (the goal line left out), the totals, and a wall of 50 squares. Mara pledged 3 and delivered them: her block is three filled squares, the first carrying a badge and her name, each linking to her pledge. Rin's 1 is an outlined square. Sam's 2.5 is two squares, and "Also: sam" under the wall counts the half. A newcomer's pledge, waiting for approval, comes last, dashed and marked Pending, outside the total. A signed-in member presses "3" beside the pledge field, posts, and lands back on `/support/` with their block. On the thread, "Open the wall" leads to the page, and the page's "Open as a thread" leads back.
Sources: [controller](../../src/ForumRewrite/Http/SupportPageController.php), [wall](../../src/ForumRewrite/View/SupportWall.php), [page](../../templates/pages/support.php), [wall partial](../../templates/partials/support_wall.php), [stylesheet](../../public/assets/support.css), [presets](../../public/assets/pledge_field.js), [site profiles](../../src/ForumRewrite/SiteProfileRegistry.php) (`supportThread`), [reply return](../../src/ForumRewrite/Http/ComposeAndAccountKeyController.php) (`resolveComposeReplyReturnTo`), [tests](../../tests/SupportPageTest.php) and [wall tests](../../tests/SupportWallTest.php), [browser check](../../tests/browser/support_wall.mjs), [plans](../plans/sponsor_wall/sponsor_wall_step3_development_plan.md).

Built on the [pledge campaign](./pledge_campaign.md): `PledgeCampaign::fromPosts()` decides what counts, and the wall only draws it, so the page and the thread always agree. Nothing is stored.
The page exists while the pledges flag is on and the profile names a thread. Otherwise it is not found. A named thread that doesn't exist yet shows only the title. It renders live, never baked, so a pledge or an approval shows on the next view. The thread page is baked, so its "Open the wall" link appears or goes after a rebuild.
Squares: one per unit of the campaign's goal ($100 each on zenmemes). A counted pledge is one block of its whole units, in thread order, with delivered squares first. A withdrawn pledge keeps what was delivered on it, as the total does. What is left of a square is counted in the total and listed under "Also:", not drawn. Pledges with no name or no readable number aren't on the wall. The wall has at least the goal's squares and grows ten at a time. A line marks the goal's last square. Rows are 10 squares, or 5 under 30rem.
Form: the campaign's own pledge form, with `return_to=/support/` (accepted only for the support thread) and preset buttons 1, 3, 5 and 10 drawn by `pledge_field.js`. Without JavaScript there are no presets, and the field still posts and returns. A guest's pledge has no name and never counts; requiring a name to pledge is a later cycle.
Naming: the page says "support" and "supporters" because "sponsor" already means vouching for a new member's key in account approval.
