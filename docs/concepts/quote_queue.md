# Quote queue

Purpose: Keep new qdb quotes off the main pages until an operator approves them, as the original qdb.us and bash.org queue did, while the queue itself stays public.
Scenario: An operator switches on `FORUM_QDB_QUEUE_ENABLED` in Tools → Feature Flags, which writes a signed change record. A visitor then adds a quote. `/api/create_quote` answers `moderation=pending`; the quote shows on `/queue` and at its own address with "Pending approval", and nowhere else. In a browser holding an operator key, the Queue page shows Approve and Reject. Approve writes a signed `approved` tag and the quote joins Latest at its number; Reject writes `rejected` and the quote leaves every page, its address included. The footer reads "N quotes approved; M pending".
Sources: [state and SQL rule](../../src/ForumRewrite/Qdb/QdbModeration.php), [start time](../../src/ForumRewrite/Security/FeatureFlagSwitchOn.php), [listing rule](../../src/ForumRewrite/ReadModel/ScoreVisibility.php), [queue page](../../src/ForumRewrite/ReadModel/QdbPage.php), [write check](../../src/ForumRewrite/Write/LocalWriteService.php) (`assertQdbModerationTag`), [operator buttons](../../templates/partials/qdb_moderation_access.php), [tests](../../tests/QdbModerationTest.php), [plans](../plans/qdb_moderated_submissions/qdb_moderated_submissions_step3_development_plan.md).
Boundary: Only quotes created at or after the first operator-signed switch-on wait; older quotes, operator quotes and `news` posts are listed at once. Without a signed switch-on nothing waits. Only signed tags from active operators count, and a rejection wins over an approval. Pending quotes are ordinary records in Git, so rejecting one hides it but does not delete it; `./v3 delete-thread` removes it. Turning the flag off lists every quote again.
