# Work claim

Purpose: Let someone who starts backlog items say so where everyone can see it, so two people or agents do not build the same item.
Scenario: The zenmemes profile names a claims thread in `backlogClaimsThread`. Ilya ticks two items on Tools → Backlog and presses Start. The start page shows the prompt at once; in the background his browser posts a signed reply in the claims thread whose first line is `claim: PF-20261104-B18, PF-20261104-B12`, and the page then reads "Claimed by ilyag since Nov 23" under each item. Both rows on the list read "Claimed by ilyag", and Hide claimed (`/backlog?claimed=hide`) leaves them out. An agent given one of those items reads `/backlog/claims.txt?items=PF-20261104-B18`, or runs `./v3 feedback claim --site=https://zenmemes.com PF-20261104-B18`, is told `Claim-State: held` by ilyag, and stops. Ilya changes his mind about one item and presses Release on its row: a reply to his claim with the line `release: PF-20261104-B12`. The other stays his until its pull request is merged and deployed, when the item is `blocked` and shows no claim.

Derived, not stored: `BacklogClaims::fromPosts()` reads the thread's replies each time the backlog is shown, as a [pledge campaign](pledge_campaign.md) reads its thread, so a rebuild cannot disagree with the posts. The backlog routes always reach PHP, so a claim shows on the next request, with high-traffic mode on or off, and needs no deploy.
The line: only a post's first line that is not blank is read. `claim:` takes one or several item IDs separated by commas or spaces; anything after the line is a free note, such as a branch name. `release` alone gives up everything the claim names; `release: <ID>` gives up those items and keeps the rest. A release is a reply to the claim it ends.
Counting: a claim counts when its post is unretracted and signed by an approved key with counted attribution. From a key not yet approved it reads "waiting for approval" and counts from the moment the key is approved. With no name it is a guest claim: "Claimed by a guest · not counted". An ID that matches no open item is ignored.
Holder: the earliest fresh counted claim holds the item; later ones read "Also claimed by NAME". A person's newest claim on an item replaces their older one. Start warns and never refuses: it names the holder and still shows the prompt.
Ending: a release by the claim's author (approved or not) or by an operator; retracting the claim; or the item becoming `blocked` or closed in the site's checkout. A counted claim reads "stale" three days after it was made, or after the item was last touched if that is later; a stale claim no longer hides the row, and a fresh claim holds ahead of it.
Who sees buttons: Claim on the start page and Release on a row are drawn for the viewer the thread page would recognise (session, else identity hint); Release also for operators on any claimed row. Both need a signing script and are hidden when scripting is off, so nobody posts a nameless claim by pressing a button. A visitor who has never posted gets the prompt and no Claim. Start claims without a tap only when the start page was reached by pressing Start; a reload or a pasted address never claims.
Agents: `/backlog.txt` adds claim lines to an item's block; `/backlog/claims.txt?items=` answers for named items with `Claim-State` `held`, `stale`, `none` or `free`; `/backlog/start.txt` names held items in `X-Claimed`; a site-made prompt names the claims address in a `Claims:` line. `./v3 feedback claim` and `release` post through Quill.
Unread: a reply in the claims thread whose whole body is one claim or release line stays out of "other new posts". It still shows in the thread and in Recent, and each claim moves the thread up the board.

Boundary: no claims thread named, no claims: the backlog pages and text routes then say nothing about them. Claims cover feedback items only; parked plans are the next cycle ([checklist](../plans/backlog_checklist.md)). The `claimed` state on posts in `#issue` threads ([tagset](tagset.md)) is a separate mark and is not read here. A checkout does not know a site's claims, so `./v3 feedback list` prints none. Behind `FORUM_BACKLOG_ENABLED`, with no flag of its own.
