# Outbox

Purpose: Keep what a person writes or likes on their own device until the server has accepted it, so losing the connection never loses work and nothing is published twice.
Scenario: Offline, a member opens a saved thread (the offline reader, a proposed concept with no entry yet), clicks Reply and writes. Post says "Saved on this device, not sent", and the reply is listed under the thread with a dashed border. When the connection returns, the page sends it through the normal prepare, sign and finalize steps, keeping the prepared post so a retry after a lost response finalizes the same post. The reply appears in the thread once, and Tools → Outbox shows it as accepted with a link.
Sources: [item states](../../public/assets/outbox_store.js), [device storage](../../public/assets/outbox_storage.js), [composer capture and queueing](../../public/assets/outbox_compose.js), [delivery](../../public/assets/outbox_sender.js), [Outbox page](../../public/assets/outbox.js), [offline compose pages](../../public/service_worker.js), [runbook](../runbooks/offline_reading.md#local-outbox), [browser test](../../tests/browser/offline_compose.mjs).
Limits: anonymous posts and images stay in the composer; a request that fails while the browser reports a connection is never queued automatically. Delivery holds a cross-tab lock, so only one tab sends at a time and a send cut off by a closed page is retried at once.
