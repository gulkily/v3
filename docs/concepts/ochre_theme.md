# Ochre theme

Purpose: A compact, text-only theme that Hacker News readers find familiar, with the reading habits they use to move through a long thread.
Scenario: An operator replies `theme: ochre` on a [ported thread](ported_thread.md). A reader who arrives from Hacker News sees a beige page and one orange bar before first paint, with small Verdana text and grey metadata lines. They press `[–]` on a long branch and it folds to `[+12]`. From a deep reply they follow "parent", then "next" to its sibling. They pick Gloss from the menu; the toggles and links disappear, and a revisit stays on Gloss.
Sources: [registry entry](../../src/ForumRewrite/View/ThemeRegistry.php) (`ochre`, light, `script`, counterpart `dark`), [site profile](../../src/ForumRewrite/SiteProfileRegistry.php) (`zenmemes` only), [stylesheet](../../public/assets/theme-ochre.css), [script](../../public/assets/theme-ochre.js), [VM test](../../tests/browser/theme_ochre_script.cjs), [browser test](../../tests/browser/theme_ochre.mjs), [theme guide](../runbooks/theme_development_guide.md#ochre).

Built on [theme script](theme_script.md) and shown on a thread through [page theme](page_theme.md). It borrows no logo or name. Every text colour meets WCAG AA: grey metadata is `#6b6b6b` (4.9:1 on the beige) and the bar's text is black (7.2:1), since white on that orange is 2.9:1. Lists number their rows with a CSS counter and leave out excerpts and thumbnails. The script reads the reply cards as they are: a branch is the run of following cards with a greater `data-reply-depth`. Folds are not stored and end on reload. With JavaScript off, Ochre is the look alone and every reply shows. Ported threads do not show Ochre by default yet; that default, and whether every site or only opted-in ones get it, is a later cycle.
