# Calendar feed

Purpose: Let people follow the calendar from Google Calendar or another calendar app, and add one event at a time, without copying anything by hand.
Scenario: A member opens `/calendar` on a phone, opens Subscribe and taps Add to Google Calendar. Google subscribes to `/calendar.ics` and, at its next refresh, shows the calendar's events: confirmed ones as confirmed, open ones as tentative. A host shares `/calendar/couches.ics` for just that tag. A reader on an event thread taps Add to Google Calendar to keep that one event, or downloads its `.ics` file for another app.
Sources: [writer](../../src/ForumRewrite/Calendar/CalendarIcs.php), [query](../../src/ForumRewrite/Calendar/CalendarQuery.php), [feed and file](../../src/ForumRewrite/Http/CalendarPageController.php), [Subscribe panel](../../templates/pages/calendar.php), [event links](../../templates/partials/event_calendar_links.php), [reference](../reference/calendar.md#subscribing), [tests](../../tests/CalendarPageTest.php), [browser test](../../tests/browser/calendar.mjs).
Boundary: Requires event support and the Calendar feed flag. Feeds list what the calendar pages list, from 90 days back; a thread's own file follows its event block instead. Times carry no zone, so apps show them as written. Feeds are public wherever the calendar is; members-only instances keep them behind sign-in, which calendar apps cannot pass. No private or per-member feeds, no time zone setting, and an event added by itself does not update.
